Our Product

Axoguard 01

A probiotic built for disease

Axoguard 01 is a rationally designed cocktail of three clinically selected bacterial strains, adapted specifically to survive and function in the gut environment of people with chronic airway disease.

Combined with an optimized prebiotic formulation, it maximizes immune-modulatory metabolite output where generic probiotics fall short. As an oral dietary supplement, it offers a faster and more accessible path to people than a traditional drug development program.

Axoguard 01 is designed to complement existing therapies such as CFTR modulators, addressing the residual gut dysbiosis they do not correct.
