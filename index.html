The Problem

An unmet need across
chronic airway disease

Pulmonary complications remain the leading cause of mortality in people with cystic fibrosis, even for those on CFTR modulators. Millions of people with CF and COPD experience gut dysbiosis and chronic pulmonary inflammation that current treatments do not adequately address.

40K

People with CF in the US

About 1,000 new diagnoses every year. 80–95% suffer chronic airway infections, and 65–85% experience chronic GI complications. Gut dysbiosis appears by 6 weeks of age and predicts future lung infection better than the airway microbiome itself.

16M+

Americans with COPD

The fourth leading cause of death globally. Gut dysbiosis is increasingly recognized as a feature of chronic airway inflammation in COPD, yet no approved product targets the gut-lung connection.

Our Mission

Why we exist

Axobiome exists to turn the emerging science of the gut–lung axis into tools to help people with chronic airway disease. We develop microbiome-based supplements and diagnostics grounded in rigorous clinical and laboratory research. Our approach pairs a disease-adapted probiotic with microbiome-based diagnostics, starting with a focus on cystic fibrosis and COPD, to help close the gap left by existing care.

Our Platform

Axoguard 01

A probiotic and diagnostic platform built for disease

Axoguard 01 is a rationally designed cocktail of three clinically selected bacterial strains, adapted specifically to survive and function in the gut environment of people with chronic airway disease.

Combined with an optimized prebiotic formulation, it maximizes immune-modulatory metabolite output where generic probiotics fall short. As an oral dietary supplement, it offers a faster and more accessible path to people than a traditional drug development program.

Axoguard 01 is paired with a companion microbiome diagnostic, also originating from the O'Toole Lab at Dartmouth, that profiles a person's gut microbial and metabolic signature. Together, the probiotic and the diagnostic form a platform: generating the data needed to understand who responds, and why, as the product moves forward.

Axoguard 01 is designed to complement existing therapies such as CFTR modulators, addressing the residual gut dysbiosis they do not correct.
